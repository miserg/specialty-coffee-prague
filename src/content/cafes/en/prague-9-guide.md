## Prague 9: The Industrial Renaissance of Specialty Coffee

While Vinohrady and Karlín traditionally monopolize Prague's third-wave cafe reputation, **Prague 9 (Vysočany and Kolbenova)** has transformed into a sanctuary for coffee purists and creatives seeking sprawling space away from central tourist crowds.

## Why Visit Kafe Pragovka?

Housed in a converted industrial hall from the former ČKD engineering works, **Kafe Pragovka** represents the pinnacle of Prague's post-industrial coffee culture:
1. **Unrivaled Acoustics and Space**: Ample room between tables make it the ideal sanctuary for remote deep-work sessions.
2. **Precision Water Filtration**: Reverse-osmosis mineralization dialed specifically to highlight delicate floral notes in high-grown Ethiopian beans.
3. **Rotating Guest Roasters**: Alongside permanent selections from Nordbeans, expect Scandinavian and German guest roasters monthly.
