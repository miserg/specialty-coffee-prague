## Prague's Specialty Coffee Revolution

Prague has rapidly emerged as one of the most vibrant specialty coffee capitals in Central Europe. Historic cobblestone streets now house cutting-edge espresso bars featuring equipment from Slayer, Synesso, and La Marzocco.

| **Onesip Coffee** | Old Town (Náměstí Republiky) | Round Hill Roastery (UK) | **Slayer Espresso 2gr** | Tiny stand-up bar, impeccable flat whites |

## Travel & Coffee Tips

- **Payment**: All specialty cafes in Prague accept contactless cards and mobile wallets. Cash is rarely needed.
- **Plant Milk**: **Oatly Barista** is the ubiquitous standard throughout the city.
- **Batch Brew**: Excellent filter coffee typically costs between 75 CZK and 95 CZK (€3.00–€3.80).
