## Hybrid Extraction in Hario Switch

The **Hario Switch** combines the best of both pour-over (percolation) and immersion brewing methods:
- **Percolation**: Highlights bright floral and citrus acidity.
- **Immersion**: Enhances sweetness, body, and compensates for uneven kettle pours.

## Step-by-Step Brewing Protocol

### 1. Preparation
1. Rinse the paper filter thoroughly with hot water with the valve open.
2. Discard rinse water, close valve, and add 18g of medium-ground coffee.

### 2. Percolation Phase (0:00 – 0:45)
1. Ensure the valve is **open**.
2. Pour **140ml of water** (93°C) in circular spirals within 30 seconds.
3. Allow water to drain through the coffee bed until 0:45.

### 3. Immersion Phase (0:45 – 2:00)
1. **Close the valve** by flipping the switch up.
2. Pour the remaining **150ml of water** (total weight: 290g).
3. Let coffee steep until the 2:00 mark.

### 4. Final Drawdown (2:00 – 2:45)
1. **Open the switch** to let the brew flow down.
2. Extraction finishes around 2:35–2:45. Swirl the server and enjoy!
