## The Science of Water-First Immersion

Traditional pour-overs drop dry coffee onto a dry filter, which often creates channeling and pulls fines to the bottom pores. The **Water-First** (reverse immersion) method eliminates this issue entirely:

- **Thermal stability**: Water retains temperature in the glass cone before ground contact.
- **No filter clogging**: Fines float initially rather than getting immediately sucked into the paper pores.
- **Ultra-uniform wetting**: Every coffee ground is surrounded by water simultaneously.

## Step-by-Step Brewing Protocol

### 1. Preparation & Preheating
1. Place filter in Hario Switch, pre-rinse with boiling water with the valve open.
2. Close the switch valve completely.

### 2. Water Pour (0:00)
1. Pour **200g of water at 92°C** directly into the closed brewer.

### 3. Add Coffee & Gentle Agitation (0:15)
1. Evenly scatter **15g of ground coffee** across the water surface.
2. With a bamboo stirrer, gently submerge any dry floating grounds with 2 gentle circular swirls.

### 4. Immersion Steep (0:30 – 2:00)
1. Let the slurry steep undisturbed until the timer hits 2:00.
2. Pour the remaining **40g of water** to rinse any crust edges.

### 5. Release Drawdown (2:15 – 2:40)
1. Press the switch switch down to open the valve.
2. The coffee drains in approximately 20–25 seconds with a flat, uniform bed. Enjoy!
